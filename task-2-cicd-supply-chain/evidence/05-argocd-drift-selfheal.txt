The Application sets syncPolicy.automated.selfHeal=true, so git is the
source of truth and anything else is drift.

NOTE ON WHAT IS DRIFTED, AND WHY IT IS NOT REPLICAS.
An earlier version of this demo scaled the Deployment and reported
success while nothing was reverted. The Application deliberately sets:

    ignoreDifferences:
      - group: apps
        kind: Deployment
        jsonPointers: [/spec/replicas]

so that an autoscaler and ArgoCD do not fight over the replica count.
Replicas are therefore the one field ArgoCD is told to ignore, and
drifting it proves nothing. This demo changes the container image
instead: the classic 'someone hotfixed production by hand' change, and
one ArgoCD very much does watch.
