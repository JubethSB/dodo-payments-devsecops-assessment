WHY THE IDENTITY FIELDS ARE THE POINT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Anyone can obtain a Fulcio certificate and sign anything, so 'this image
  is signed' means nothing by itself. What matters is the Subject above: the
  signature is bound to one workflow file, on one ref, in one repository.
  Verification pins that identity AND the OIDC issuer, which is exactly what
  Task 1's Kyverno policy checks at admission.

  No signing key exists to steal. The private key lived for the duration of
  the job and was never written to disk.

  Note also what the signature does NOT say: nothing about CVEs. That is the
  scan gate's job, which is why it runs first and separately.
