WHY THIS SPLIT DECIDES THE POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  33 findings have NO fix available. Blocking on them would leave
  this pipeline permanently red with no action that could turn it
  green - so the gate would be disabled or bypassed within a week,
  and every FIXABLE finding would then ship unnoticed too.

  The blocking step therefore runs with --ignore-unfixed: the 149
  fixable findings hard-block, the unfixed ones are reported loudly
  and triaged out of band (reachability, compensating control,
  named owner, review date, continuous re-scan).
