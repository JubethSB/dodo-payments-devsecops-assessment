----------------------------------------------------------------------
ISSUANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The injector mounts a PROJECTED ServiceAccount token into the
     sidecar with audience 'istio-ca'. Note this works even though the
     workloads set automountServiceAccountToken: false - that field
     only suppresses the default kube-api-access volume, and Istio
     mounts its own, narrowly-audienced one.
  2. pilot-agent generates a private key IN THE POD. The key is never
     transmitted; only a CSR leaves the sidecar.
  3. istiod validates the token via the TokenReview API, confirming the
     pod really runs under that ServiceAccount, and signs the CSR.
  4. The certificate is delivered over SDS and held in memory. It is
     not written to disk and not stored in a Secret, so there is no
     at-rest copy for an attacker with etcd or volume access to steal.

  The SAN is the SPIFFE ID the AuthorizationPolicy matches on:
    spiffe://cluster.local/ns/payments/sa/reporting

----------------------------------------------------------------------
ROTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Default lifetime is 24h and pilot-agent renews at ~50% of it, so
  roughly every 12h, without restarting the pod. Short lifetimes are
  the reason there is no CRL here: revocation is achieved by declining
  to renew, which bounds the damage from a leaked key to hours rather
  than relying on every peer honouring a revocation list.

  This is also why the NetworkPolicy in networkpolicy/00-default-deny
  must permit egress to istiod on 15012. Block it and nothing fails
  immediately - existing certs keep working - and then the namespace
  fails closed hours later when renewal is due.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
TRUST ROOT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  istiod acts as the CA. On a fresh install it self-generates a root
  and stores it in the istio-ca-secret Secret in istio-system:
    istio-ca-secret type=istio.io/ca-root keys=
    key:     "ca-cert.pem"
    "ca-key.pem"
    "cert-chain.pem"
    "root-cert.pem"

  The root public cert is distributed to every namespace in the
  istio-ca-root-cert ConfigMap, which is how a sidecar validates its
  peers:
    payments/istio-ca-root-cert

  HONEST LIMITATION for a PCI deployment: a self-signed root generated
  by istiod means the CA private key lives in a Kubernetes Secret,
  readable by anyone with get-secrets in istio-system, and compromising
  it forges ANY workload identity in the mesh. Production should make
  istiod an intermediate under an offline or HSM-backed root, so the
  blast radius of a cluster compromise is one revocable intermediate
  rather than the whole trust domain.
